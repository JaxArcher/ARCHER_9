## Locked / strongly established
- Seven Council seats: five active, two memorial.
- Fallen Families survived and became Lost Order.
- Seiyrah is the First Family descendant, not Techana.
- Aldren and Schyne are not married in Book One.
- Royal triad was Queen's chosen design.
- Aldren's biological father died from cumulative active-Blur magical training in armor.
- Research Center and Magic Council are separate.
- Ordinary displaced Court creatures can possess full agency.
- Blur-born Primal creatures remain aware despite compulsion.
- Varek surrenders after Rouzen dies.
- Techana secretly allows Varek to return to Primal Court.
- Techana releases dragon selflessly; it returns voluntarily and Realized.
- King's Highway booster is mechanical.
- Lost Order alloy is a partial, ethically complicated solution.

## Newer governing magic direction
- Magic is elemental.
- Standard human magic uses iron tattoos as detailed contracts.
- Wild casters can negotiate directly.
- Living systems have internal constraints external magic cannot casually override.
- Revised system must preserve or deliberately replace the plot functions inherited from v13.
