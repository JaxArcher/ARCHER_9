# Glossary

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Reference
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15


- **Anchor (social):** Woman in the traditional two-men/one-woman triad.
- **Blur:** Nightly reality-thinning phenomenon resisted by iron.
- **Blur-hunters:** Hunters who capture/kill emerging Court creatures for essence.
- **Composition:** Theory that depletion of core iron worsens the Blur.
- **Court creature:** Being associated with the adjacent Court/Blur system; agency depends on origin/bond, not mere species.
- **Fallen Families:** Two expelled founding families, ancestors of Lost Order.
- **First Families:** Founding political lineages.
- **Iron Council:** Iron-supply institution: five active seats plus two memorial seats.
- **Iron contract tattoo:** Newer concept: iron tattoo encoding a detailed elemental agreement.
- **King's Highway:** Crown-owned rail system moving iron.
- **Lost Order:** Hidden druid descendants of Fallen Families.
- **Magic Council:** Independent magical-material distribution body.
- **Primal:** Blur-born bond associated in v13 with selfish desire and compulsion.
- **Realized:** Blur-born relationship associated with genuine selflessness and clarity rather than compulsion.
- **Research Center:** Theocracy-run residue-processing facility with secret Court-creature activities.
- **Rootfast:** Druid-related community holding partial Composition knowledge.
- **Soul anchor:** Human linked to a Blur-born bonded Court creature.
- **Theocracy:** Religious state and major political power.

## Terms requiring reconciliation
Registry/residue ink, essence ink, Primal Court, and wild nature magic remain narratively important but some definitions need updating after the elemental-contract revision.
