# Nations and Power Structure

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Worldbuilding
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Current canon

The Crown owns the Highway and holds legitimacy; the Iron Council controls supply; the Theocracy is a religious state with major demand; the Magic Council is an independent cross-national distribution body.

## Narrative/continuity note

No institution controls every resource, making interdependence a source of both stability and vulnerability.
