# The Queen

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Role

Queen, Anchor of royal triad, Aldren's mother, Stelselgreg's closest friend, and co-architect of household structure.

## Character

Asexual. Chose the triad because it suited all three. Voice: theatrical precision, dry/deadpan wisdom, emotionally incisive.

## History

Met Stelselgreg as young infirmary nurse after his war injury; helped rebuild relationship with Aldren's bio-father and design triad.

## Court creatures

Self-identifies as mother to parentless Court creatures and becomes morally important once exploitation is known.

## Magic

Wild/unrefined nature magic. New direction: direct real-time elemental negotiation rather than pre-inscribed iron contract. Flexible, not automatically stronger.

## Visual continuity

Curly auburn hair, gray-blue eyes, mature age near Stelselgreg, structured dark teal dress in established look, no visible iron-contract tattoos; use latest locked royal-family face.

## System significance

Proof that iron tattoos are one method/interface, not the only possible source of magic.
