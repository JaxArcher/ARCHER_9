# Techana

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Role

Theocracy-raised operative embedded with Seiyrah at an outer-region iron distribution hub. Child of executed druids, though raised on a false soldier-death story.

## Psychology

Secure attachment; primary wound is epistemic. When inherited authority proves morally corrupt, Techana stops trusting institutional certainty.

## Defense / tell

Moral paralysis. He would rather absorb visible cost himself than make an ethically uncertain choice.

## Dragon arc

Historically used dragon without question. Later refuses to use it because consent/righteousness cannot be verified. Varek's testimony lets him reason from lived truth; he releases dragon selflessly and it returns voluntarily, Realized.

## Book Two

Deeper guilt is not only later paralysis but years of unquestioned obedience. Schyne's 'monster keeper' accusation forces responsibility.

## Sibling

Younger twin; Seiyrah historically protects him. Lost Order tragedy triggers his first decisive protective turn toward her.

## Visual continuity

Use locked identity. Current travel direction includes short dark hair, light facial hair, practical chainmail/faded blue tunic, no random neck tattoos. Dragon tattoo imagery developed over right chest/shoulder/upper arm.

## Open

How inborn druid capability maps to revised elemental system; whether Varek release becomes public.
