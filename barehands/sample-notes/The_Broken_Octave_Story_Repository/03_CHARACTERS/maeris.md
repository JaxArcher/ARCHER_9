# Maeris

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Role

Rouzen's wife and Schyne's mother. Emotionally neglected by Rouzen and genuinely loved by Varek.

## Key beats

Rouzen blames her for dynasty collapse; she flees to Varek; Varek promises Rouzen will not hurt her that way again; she later dies.

## Death

Cause deliberately ambiguous. Do not convert to a solved murder without explicit decision.

## Narrative role

Makes Rouzen's harm domestic/intimate and complicates easy readings of Varek's constrained love.
