# Schyne

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Canonical role

Son of Rouzen and Maeris, raised in practice largely by Varek.

## Magical origin

Carries part of Varek's essence through Rouzen's bloodline. Magic is natural-born, untattooed, and unregistered; Theocracy law treats it like forbidden witchcraft though it is not druid magic.

## Psychology

Secure baseline from Varek's warm, consistent caregiving. Present crisis is a crisis of meaning: was the relationship that made him secure freely chosen if Varek lived under compulsion?

## Key beats

Supports Aldren; announces union intent; discovers Rouzen's evidence; briefly overpowers Rouzen; is thrown into Blur; encounters Lireth; returns after subjective seconds while months passed; later confronts Techana over releasing Varek.

## Relationships

Aldren — partner; Lireth — Realized metaphysical bond that must become chosen relationship; Varek — primary caregiver/paternal figure; Rouzen — controlling biological father; Maeris — mother.

## Open

When/how Schyne learns the full origin of his magic and whether he learns Rouzen/Varek always knew; how natural magic works under revised system.
