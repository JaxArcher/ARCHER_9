# Rouzen

**Status:** LOCKED
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Role

Iron Council member and principal personal antagonist of Book One.

## Developmental psychology

Tier 1: scarce, conditional care. Tier 2: sudden affection/status when named presumptive heir teaches status=love. Tier 3: loss of inheritance, betrothal, and social standing confirms the rule at adult stakes.

## Core schema

Worth, status, and love are inseparable; loss becomes betrayal.

## Motivator / defense

Controls uncertainty to prevent betrayal. Performs in public and externalizes blame. Most unstable when privately known accurately.

## Conspiracy

Uses a corrupted Magic Council contact and routine Research Center reporting to obtain core-iron movement data, then manipulates allocation and Blur intensity while appearing expert.

## Varek / Schyne

Varek forms during Rouzen's Iron Trials crisis and shares his grievance. Rouzen knows Schyne carries Varek's essence.

## End

Killed in Book One final confrontation.

## Open by design

Possible role in deaths connected to his housefather's later family remains ambiguous.
