# Davan

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Role

Aldren's guard and closest friend; large formidable human, specifically not Goliath.

## Psychology

Tier 1: general work-ethic household. Tier 2: adopts Council prestige as a socially prescribed goal. Tier 3: heiress chooses two other men after he believed he had won; adult betrayal is the actual wound.

## Redefinition

Learning Aldren sought to relinquish privilege rather than accumulate it converts contempt into deep respect.

## Motivator / defense

Wants to be genuinely needed and valued. Converts fear/love into anger, scolding, and physical intervention.

## Payoff

Takes Lireth's chain from Aldren without being asked, absorbing danger so Aldren can fight.

## Visual continuity

Large human, formidable, not giant-kin. Use locked face where available. Training look: rugged black fitted trousers, heavy boots, reinforced belt, wrist wraps, bare chest.

## Open

Final surname; dedicated Book Two arc or ensemble-support role.
