# Varek

**Status:** LOCKED
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Nature

Blur-born Primal Court creature bonded to Rouzen as soul anchor; fully self-aware despite compulsion.

## Origin

Forms during Rouzen's lowest moment at the Iron Trials and initially shares Rouzen's grievance that he was betrayed.

## Compulsion

Cannot simply refuse binding desire/command, but where latitude exists he chooses the shape of compliance. Direct commands later prove especially restrictive.

## Schyne and Maeris

Genuinely loves both. Varek's essence passes through Rouzen into Schyne, making the connection partly literal.

## Schyne's fall

When forced to throw Schyne into the Blur, Varek chooses the version most likely to preserve him.

## Resolution

After Rouzen dies Varek surrenders, avoids Schyne's gaze, discusses authentic love with Techana, and asks not to remain as perpetual pain for Schyne. Techana secretly lets him return to the Primal Court.

## Open

How widely Techana's unauthorized release becomes known.
