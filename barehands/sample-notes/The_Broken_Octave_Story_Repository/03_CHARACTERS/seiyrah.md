# Seiyrah

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Role

Techana's heteropaternal twin and older/protective sibling in practice; Theocracy-raised operative.

## Psychology

Caregiver-secure but identity-fused with being the protector. Her wound is learning that competence and loyalty did not guarantee she was protecting something worthy.

## Defense / tell

Caretaking action: heals, organizes, and protects others before herself.

## Book Two ancestry

Lost Order recognizes Seiyrah—not Techana—as descendant of a Fallen First Family. She finds real belonging, then loses most of that lineage to Theocracy attack.

## Arc function

Her grief reverses the sibling support pattern and requires Techana to become protector.

## Visual continuity

Use locked face/identity from latest references. Wilderness/rail clothing practical to era; avoid unexplained facial markings and excess tattooing.

## Status note

v13 explicitly says this profile is less pressure-tested than others.
