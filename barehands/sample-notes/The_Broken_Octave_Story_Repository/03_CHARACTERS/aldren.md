# Aldren

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Character
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Canonical role

Crown Prince and heir. Son of the Queen and Stelselgreg's male partner / Aldren's biological father. Stelselgreg named him heir from birth.

## Psychology

Tier 1 is secure and genuinely loved. In adolescence Aldren misreads Stelselgreg's grief as guilt and concludes he must earn an independent Council legacy to discharge a debt. Chapters 13–14 resolve that specific belief; a softer habit remains that legitimacy feels most real when demonstrated through service and costly action.

## Motivator / defense

Before resolution: earn worth independent of birthright. After resolution: fiercely protect people he loves. Defense is dry humor under severe pressure; behavioral tell is reckless self-endangerment.

## Relationships

Stelselgreg — father/king; Queen — mother; biological father — deceased legacy figure; Schyne — romantic partner; Lireth — genuine closeness during Schyne's absence; Davan — closest friend; Techana/Seiyrah — people Aldren first condemns under manipulated evidence and later risks himself to save.

## Magic

Accomplished caster with high-risk teleportation capable of carrying others through/around a Blur crisis. Teleportation needs earlier seeding in Book One.

## Visual continuity

Royal formal language: white-and-gold, medieval-to-slightly-Victorian. Use latest locked royal-family face/age/build. Training wardrobe is separate from court wardrobe.

## Open

Long-range leadership philosophy; final timing of Aldren–Schyne–Lireth union.
