# The Blur and the Composition

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Subplot
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Current thread

The Blur is the nightly reality-thinning event resisted by iron. The Composition reverses civilization's core assumption: extracting iron to survive may itself worsen the Blur. Book One assembles clues; Book Two's Lost Order confirms the theory. The alloy reduces raw-iron demand but does not automatically solve the social/ecological problem.
