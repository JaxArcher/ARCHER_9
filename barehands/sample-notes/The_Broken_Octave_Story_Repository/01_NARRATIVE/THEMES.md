# Themes

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Narrative
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Survival systems become belief systems

A tool built from necessity can accumulate doctrine, prestige, and moral authority until questioning it feels like questioning civilization.

## Inherited certainty versus earned judgment

Techana most directly embodies the problem of what to do after inherited authority proves unreliable.

## Compulsion does not erase personhood

Varek and the dragon separate constrained action from absence of self.

## Love chosen versus love obligated

The royal triad, Schyne/Varek, and Aldren/Schyne/Lireth all test whether affection remains meaningful when duty, structure, or magic are present.

## Prestige, legitimacy, visible effort

Rouzen, Aldren, and Davan respond differently to a culture in which worth is socially proved.

## Extraction versus reciprocity

The Composition literalizes the cost of a civilization that protects itself by taking from the world.

## Truth destabilizes

Revelations destroy stabilizing identities and institutions as well as exposing lies.
