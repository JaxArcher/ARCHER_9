# Narrative Structure

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Narrative
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Book One — Establish, fracture, expose

Establish the world strongly enough that later revelations have something real to break. Move from local irregularities to systemic deception.

## Book Two — Consequence, origin, escalation

Begin after the personal villain falls. Make characters live with the damage, then reveal the deeper historical/ecological cause and allow the Theocracy to become an overt strategic threat.

## Structural rule for revelations

A major reveal should reinterpret an earlier scene, force changed behavior, or alter what solutions are available. If it does none of these, it is probably lore rather than plot.

## Watchpoint

The Book Two ending needs a deliberate emotional shape after resolving the union-timing conflict.
