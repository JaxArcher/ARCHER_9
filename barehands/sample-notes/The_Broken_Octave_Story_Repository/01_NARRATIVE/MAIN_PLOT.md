# Main Plot

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Narrative
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Phase 1 — Apparent coherence

Crown, Council, Theocracy, Magic Council, King's Highway, iron distribution, and tattoo magic appear complicated but functional. Personal conflicts initially look local.

## Phase 2 — Hidden manipulation

Techana and Seiyrah's investigation and Schyne's discovery expose Rouzen's private use of core-iron data to manipulate allocation and Blur intensity. Rouzen turns genuine evidence of espionage against the twins.

## Phase 3 — Old explanations break

The Iron Trials rescue, Research Center raid, Court-creature evidence, Rootfast, and Pact of Five reveal that public truths about magic, creatures, the Fallen Families, and the Blur are incomplete or false.

## Phase 4 — Rouzen falls; system remains

Rouzen dies, but Varek and the dragon force the party to confront agency, compulsion, responsibility, and love. The eight-person party forms without solving the world.

## Phase 5 — Book Two exposes the ecological cause

The Lost Order confirms that removing iron from the world's core worsens the Blur. Its alloy is a partial solution, not a clean fix.

## Phase 6 — Institutional conflict becomes open conflict

The Theocracy destroys much of the Lost Order, repurposes Rouzen's corrupted information channel, and strikes the King's Highway and iron reserves.

## Long-range state

The story points toward replacement of the inherited order rather than simple restoration. The exact political and magical settlement remains open.
