# Timeline

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Narrative chronology
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Deep history

Seven founding families form the early order. Two identify the self-reinforcing iron problem and are expelled by the other five. Their seats remain empty; they survive as the Lost Order.

## Royal previous generation

Stelselgreg and Aldren's biological father serve together. The bio-father rescues Stelselgreg. The future Queen nurses Stelselgreg and later helps create the triad. Aldren is conceived within that chosen family and named heir from birth. His biological father later dies from cumulative active-Blur magical training while armored.

## Rouzen

Conditional childhood care becomes status-tied approval when he is named presumptive heir; a later blood heir displaces him. After further losses, he enters the Iron Trials and Varek manifests.

## Techana and Seiyrah

Their druid parents are executed by the Theocracy. The twins are raised on a false soldier-death story, trained as operatives, and placed at a distribution hub.

## Book One milestones

Council/Highway investigation → twins enter capital → Maeris dies → study conspiracy uncovered → Schyne enters Blur → twins condemned → Aldren rescues them → Research Center/Rootfast discoveries → Rouzen defeated → Schyne/Lireth return → Varek released → dragon released and returns Realized → eight-person party complete.

## Book Two milestones

Post-Rouzen fallout → Lost Order search → Seiyrah lineage → Composition confirmed → alloy revealed/withheld → Lost Order raid → Techana confession → relational repair begins → Theocracy poisons logistics → Highway/reserve seizure.
