# Story Premise

**Status:** ACTIVE / DEVELOPING
**Last reconciled:** 2026-09-15
**Component type:** Narrative
**Source basis:** Story Bible v13 + newer project decisions through 2026-09-15

## Core premise

Human civilization survives the nightly Blur because iron stabilizes reality against it. That dependence has become political infrastructure: the Crown owns the rail network, the Iron Council controls supply, and the Theocracy interprets the Blur through religious doctrine.

The survival system is also part of the problem. Continued extraction of iron from the world's core appears to worsen the Blur. Rouzen exploits information asymmetries to manipulate iron distribution and Blur intensity, while deeper history shows earlier leaders rejected a self-costly solution and expelled the families who proposed it.

## One-sentence version

As a civilization's iron-based defense against a nightly reality-collapse quietly worsens the catastrophe it prevents, a royal heir, defecting operatives, and people bound to the magical system uncover the founding betrayal beneath their world and must decide what survival is worth sacrificing.

## Thematic promise

The story is not simply 'the institutions were evil.' Several systems solved real survival problems. The drama is what happens when inherited solutions become self-perpetuating, ethically corrosive, and too structurally important to abandon cleanly.
