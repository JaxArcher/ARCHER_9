## Rule
The repository treats **The Iron Trials — Story Bible v13 (2026-07-11)** as the baseline, then applies newer project decisions through **2026-09-15**.

When newer material contradicts v13, the newer decision governs. Older material is retained under legacy/superseded sections when it remains useful.

## Canon versus analysis
- **Canonical Facts / Current Canon** = explicitly established and not superseded.
- **Development Analysis** = interpretation, structural diagnosis, or extrapolation not yet adopted.
- **Open Questions** = intentionally unresolved.

## Major post-v13 override zone
The largest override concerns the magic system. v13 describes mastery-based tattoos plus registry/essence ink. Later work reframes ordinary human magic as **elemental negotiation mediated through iron tattoos whose designs are detailed contracts/specifications**. The repository preserves v13 mechanics where plot depends on them but marks the underlying explanation for reconciliation.
