# Status Legend

- **LOCKED** — Established canon. Do not change without deliberately reopening it.
- **ACTIVE / DEVELOPING** — Current usable direction; details may still change.
- **OPEN** — Deliberately unresolved. Do not invent a solution downstream.
- **PROPOSED** — Discussed but not adopted.
- **CONFLICT / NEEDS RESOLUTION** — Two live/inherited ideas disagree.
- **SUPERSEDED** — Older idea replaced by a newer decision.

## Chapter drafting states
- **OUTLINED** — Story function/events known; prose not treated as written.
- **PARTIAL OUTLINE** — Only some scene beats are established/recovered.
- **PLACEHOLDER** — Narrative function known; scene still needs design.
- **NEEDS SOURCE VERIFICATION** — Chapter exists but exact title/full beats were not recoverable.

## Precedence
1. Newer explicit project decisions override older Story Bible material.
2. Story Bible v13 remains canon where no newer decision conflicts.
3. Development analysis is not canon unless explicitly adopted.
4. Conflicts are preserved and flagged rather than silently merged.
